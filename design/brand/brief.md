# Camber identity exploration

Camber turns a phone into a private local Windows webcam. The shared mark must
work on Android adaptive icons, Windows taskbars, and monochrome tray icons.
Direction: calm, rounded, approachable, precise. Avoid camera clip art, shutter
blades, Wi-Fi arcs, speech bubbles and a generic blue technology badge.

Word map: camber > curvature, bridge, lift; camera > aperture, opening, view;
connection > handoff, continuity, joining; privacy > personal space, enclosure.

Concept longlist: open C with an arriving point; paired rounded windows;
arched bridge; rising horizon; inset doorway; offset viewfinder corners; shared
negative-space lens; soft cb monogram; two orbiting surfaces; folded ribbon C.

Shortlist: A letterform C plus arriving point (clearest name recognition);
B abstract paired arches (connection without drawing hardware);
C opposing rounded view corners (distinctive compact app silhouette).
These are exploratory original marks, not trademark-cleared identities.

Construction: 256-unit grid, 32-unit main bands, round ends, generous holes.
Review in black at 16/32/64 pixels before selecting color and platform variants.
Final icons and installation follow the user's concept choice.
