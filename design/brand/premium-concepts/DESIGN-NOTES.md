# Camber premium logo exploration

Brief: a memorable, confident and polished identity for a private phone-to-PC camera utility. Native Android and Windows app icons are the primary use. Keep soft geometry and the graphite / blue palette of the latest Android interface. No new name. Current Open C + dot is simple and functional, but its detached dot and uniform circular silhouette offer limited distinction.

Avoid category shortcuts: six-blade shutters, camera outlines, Wi-Fi arcs, generic infinity links, privacy shields, and metallic gradients that disguise an ordinary shape. Metallic belongs to the interface material; the mark must stand without effects.

Word map: Camber / curve / lift / bridge; phone + PC / two views / relay / shared field; premium / deliberate / sculpted / confident. Intersection: a curved bridge between two views.

Concept longlist, scored 1-5 for clarity/distinction/simplicity/relevance/small size:
1. Sculpt: a broad C with diagonal, outward-flared terminals. 5/3/5/4/5. Selected as evolutionary direction.
2. Duet: two offset, rounded corner forms interlock around a diagonal open channel. 4/4/5/5/5. Selected as abstract direction.
3. Offset: a rounded field is opened by an eccentric counter and diagonal cut. 4/4/4/4/4. Selected as bold direction.
4. Bow: a single lifted arch with a released end. 3/4/5/3/4.
5. Trace: custom lowercase c with a rising inner shelf. 4/3/4/4/4.
6. Fold: ribbon C with one turned-back corner. 4/3/3/4/3.
7. Pulse: counter-rotating arcs with unequal sweep. 3/2/4/4/3.
8. Bridge: two pillars joined by a bowed stroke. 3/3/5/4/4.
9. Viewline: parallel bands open into one shared counter. 3/3/3/4/3.
10. Camber wordmark with linked am shoulder. 3/4/3/3/2.

Geometry: symbols occupy approximately 200/256 units, consistent apparent weight and clear space. Each is built from filled outlines; no text, gradients, masks or strokes in symbols. Discovery uses the installed reference library for category conventions and peer craft; reference forms are not copied.

These are exploration files, not production replacements. App assets remain unchanged until a direction is chosen. No trademark clearance is claimed.
